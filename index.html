EYE MAZE

Steer a point of light through the maze with your eyes.

  1. Look left, right, up or down — the ball rolls that way.
  2. Look at the middle of the screen — the ball stops.
  3. No setup: allow the camera and the maze begins.

Your camera is processed locally in this browser by an on-device face model. Video is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Works best with your face evenly lit and the webcam roughly at eye level. Test mode (keyboard, no camera)

Camera access

Your browser will ask to use the camera. Choose Allow.
